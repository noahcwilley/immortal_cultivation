# Immortal Cultivation

Immortal Cultivation adds cultivation to Crusader Kings III. Seek out a master, refine your Qi, and extend your life as you progress through cultivation realms.

You can withdraw into seclusion to practice steadily, or take your chances with opportunities in the world. Absorbing a mote of wild Qi can advance your cultivation, but a failed attempt can be fatal.

## Features

- Find a cultivation master and begin at Qi Condensation.
- Develop your Subrealm and Quality through separate trait tracks.
- Enter seclusion to cultivate at the expense of your worldly affairs.
- Track your progress through a seclusion story in the Situations menu.
- Gain lifespan and prowess as your cultivation improves.
- Encounter wild Qi, with success chances influenced by your cultivation.

## Getting started

Enable the mod in your CK3 playset, then take the **Search for a Master** decision. You can also choose a cultivation trait in Ruler Designer.

## Development

The mod is in early development and currently focuses on Qi Condensation. Foundation Establishment is partially implemented, with more realms and cultivation encounters planned.

**Supported CK3 version:** 1.20.1.2  
**Language:** English
